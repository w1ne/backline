Backline

You play. A band in your browser hears your tempo and key and plays along. Drums, bass, keys and a lead line, each one you can switch on or off while you play.

50 hours5+ peopleweb browsermic or MIDI inpaid APIs allowed

1. The demo

One of us plays a riff. Drums come in, then bass, then keys, then a lead answers. Genre flips from lofi to funk mid-jam. Under two minutes.

2. How a session goes

  1. Pick input and genreMic or MIDI. Lofi, funk, rock, jazz.
  2. Play four barsApp locks: "112 BPM · A minor".
  3. Tap instruments in and out, set CreativityChanges land on the next bar.
  4. Stop, replay, downloadMix plus one file per instrument.

5. How it works

ListenerMic or MIDI → beat tracker + key detector. Output: BPM, key, notes right now.
→
BandleaderA clock ticking at the BPM. Each bar it asks each instrument that is on for the next bar of notes, in the key, in the genre.
→
PlayersNotes → sound in the browser. Sample-based drums, bass, keys, lead.

What we reuse (licenses checked 11 Sep)

BoxPlan A · in-browser, no networkPlan B · generative audio on top
Beat & tempoMIDI: note timestamps. Mic: BeatNet (streaming, CC-BY) or our own onset + autocorrelation over 4 bars.—
Key & notesMIDI: note numbers. Mic: chroma histogram → key; BTC (MIT) if we want chords. Not basic-pitch: over 1 s latency.—
Generating partsGenre pattern banks, transposed to key, with variation. Optional: Anticipatory Music Transformer (Apache-2.0 code+weights) for melody→accompaniment infilling of the next bar.Magenta RealTime 2 (CC-BY weights, ~200 ms, takes our MIDI as conditioning; needs Apple Silicon) or Lyria RealTime via Gemini API (bpm/scale/mute controls, ~2 s, no audio input; paid tier only, free tier trains on user audio).
Making soundTone.js or SpessaSynth with CC0 samples/soundfonts, one set per genre.Model streams audio directly.
ExportRecord each instrument bus in the browser. Later "upload a track" mode: Demucs (MIT) + ACE-Step 1.5 complete (MIT code+weights) to add stems offline.Same.
Measured 11 Sep: Lyria RealTime delivers ~0.8× real time on our paid key (stalls every few seconds; the player loops the last bar to cover) and reacts to changes in ~0.5 s with a reset+crossfade. Access goes through our relay (GitHub sign-in, collaborators only; the API key never reaches the browser). Because of the rate, the live engine will be self-hosted on a cheap GPU: ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT; hears the player's audio, regenerates the next 4 bars one bar ahead) is being benchmarked first, Magenta RealTime 2 (streaming, ~200 ms) second. The pattern engine stays as the no-network baseline. Avoid MusicGen, JASCO, YuE, madmom and Essentia models: non-commercial weights.

7. Risks

  • Mic bleed from speakers. Headphones; lock tempo once, stop re-estimating.
  • Loud venue. MIDI keyboard is the primary demo input, mic the second.
  • Browser latency. Test on the demo laptop on day one.
  • Sounds like a toy. Sample quality beats model quality; spend time on sounds.
